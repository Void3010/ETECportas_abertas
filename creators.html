IT Content Creator

Role Education Salary Application

Role

The IT Content Creator is responsible for developing and producing content related to Information Technology, such as videos, live streams, tutorials, articles, and posts on digital platforms.

Education

There are several educational backgrounds that contribute to working as a Content Creator, such as Systems Development, Information Technology, Computer Science, Design, Communication, and Audiovisual Production. Knowledge of video editing, web development, graphic design, and digital tools is also important.

Salary

Compensation varies according to experience, education, specialization, position, company, and location. The estimated salary for professionals hired under the CLT employment system ranges from R$ 3,000 to R$ 8,000 per month, but if the service is provided independently, it can exceed R$ 15,000 per month.

Application

The professional can work in technology companies, startups, software companies, educational institutions, communication agencies, digital platforms, and independent projects.
