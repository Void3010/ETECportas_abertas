IT Content Manager

Role Education Salary Application

Role

The IT Content Manager is responsible for planning, organizing, managing, and supervising digital content. Their activities include defining publishing strategies, organizing calendars, managing platforms, and analyzing performance metrics.

Education

There are several educational backgrounds that contribute to working as a Content Manager, such as Marketing, Communication, Information Technology, Information Systems, Systems Development, and Business Administration. Knowledge of digital marketing, data analysis, CEO, and platform management is also relevant.

Salary

Compensation varies according to experience, education, specialization, position, company, and location. The estimated salary ranges from R$ 4,500 to R$ 10,000 per month.

Application

The professional can work in technology companies, startups, software companies, marketing agencies, educational institutions, digital platforms, and communication and content teams.
