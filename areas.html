Areas of Expertise and Compensation

Discover where digital professionals work and current market salary ranges.

Content Creator

Where They Work:

Technology companies, startups, marketing/communication agencies, video production studios, digital platforms, and independent ventures (Creator Economy).

Salary Ranges:
  • Junior / Video Editor: R$ 2,500 to R$ 4,000 /month
  • Mid-Level / Videomaker: R$ 4,500 to R$ 7,500 /month
  • Senior / Coordinator: R$ 8,000 to R$ 14,000+ /month
  • Freelance / Independent Creator: R$ 3,000 to R$ 15,000+ /month (via sponsorships and partnerships)

Content Manager

Where They Work:

SaaS startups, enterprise corporations, financial institutions, performance marketing agencies, and strategic consultancies.

Salary Ranges:
  • Junior Manager: R$ 3,500 to R$ 5,000 /month
  • Mid-Level Manager: R$ 6,000 to R$ 9,500 /month
  • Senior Manager / Head of Content: R$ 11,000 to R$ 22,000+ /month
